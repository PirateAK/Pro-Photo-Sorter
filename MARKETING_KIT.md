## 5 · Pricing psychology (my two cents)

- **$29 lifetime** is the sweet spot — cheap enough to impulse-buy, expensive enough to signal quality. Photo Mechanic is $150. Lightroom is $9.99/mo forever.
- **Add a "Pro Bundle" at $59** with a bonus (starter tag pack collection: Wedding, Wildlife, Sports, Portrait, etc.) — anchoring gets people to buy the $29 base without a second thought.
- **Never do "free forever" with a paid upgrade.** Your Trial mode with `_TRIAL` suffix is a stroke of genius — it lets people use every feature without watermarking their work, but the annoyance factor drives conversion. Keep it exactly as-is.
